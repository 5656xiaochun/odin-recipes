Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a layered pasta dish made with sauce, cheese, and filling.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ground beef
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the lasagna noodles.
  2. Cook the ground beef.
  3. Add the tomato sauce.
  4. Layer the noodles, sauce, and cheese.
  5. Bake until the cheese is melted.
